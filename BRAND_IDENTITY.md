## Brand Language Guidelines

### DO ✅
- Use "CallSync" (not "Call Sync" or "callsync")
- Emphasize time-saving and revenue growth
- Talk about "conversations" and "customer experience"
- Use inclusive language (all business sizes)
- Be specific about results ("200% faster scheduling", "15 min to deploy")

### DON'T ❌
- Oversell AI (it's a tool, not magic)
- Use jargon without explanation (avoid "multi-tenant" in marketing)
- Make promises about call quality (Twilio does that)
- Compare to enterprise solutions (we're for SMBs)
- Use outdated terms ("chatbot", "automated robot")
